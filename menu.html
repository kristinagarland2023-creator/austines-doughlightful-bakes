Our Menu

Sourdough, sweets, and good eats

A selection of small-batch, from-scratch baked goods made with high-quality ingredients.

Cookies

Loaded Chocolate Chip Cookies

Bakery-style cookies loaded with semi-sweet chocolate chips, milk chocolate chips, and rich chocolate chunks. Finished off with a touch of flaky sea salt.

Classic Peanut Butter Cookies

Soft and chewy cookies with rich peanut butter flavor and the classic crisscross on top.

Oatmeal Cream Pie Cookies

Soft, cinnamon-kissed oatmeal cookies with a fluffy marshmallow cream filling. Warm, nostalgic, and made to feel like home.

Brownies

Chocolate Chip Fudge Brownies

Deep, fudgy chocolate brownies loaded with melty chocolate chips. Rich, comforting, and made for true chocolate lovers.

S’mores Brownies

Fudgy chocolate brownies loaded with chocolate chips and chunks, layered with graham cracker and gooey marshmallow. Like a campfire treat, made to feel like home.

Sourdough & Specialty Bakes

Sourdough Cinnamon Rolls

Soft, fluffy sourdough rolls swirled with cinnamon and brown sugar and topped with a smooth, tangy-sweet cream cheese-style frosting. Warm, gooey, and perfectly comforting.

Sourdough Boule or Loaf

Crusty, golden sourdough with a soft, airy interior and a subtle tang. Simple, rustic, and full of flavor.

Jalapeño Cheddar Sourdough Boule

Crusty sourdough packed with sharp cheddar and jalapeños. Melty, savory, and just the right kick.
